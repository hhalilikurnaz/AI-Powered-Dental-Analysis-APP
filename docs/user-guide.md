## Account Setup

### Creating Your Account

1. **Visit the Registration Page**
   - Go to `https://app.shtechlabs.com/register`
   - Click on "Create New Account"

2. **Complete Registration Form**
   - Enter your professional details
   - Provide valid dental license information
   - Set a strong password (minimum 8 characters with special characters)
   - Agree to terms and conditions

3. **Email Verification**
   - Check your email for verification link
   - Click the link to activate your account
   - Complete profile setup

4. **Initial Setup**
   - Configure your practice information
   - Set up notification preferences
   - Choose your subscription plan

### First Login

1. Navigate to `https://app.shtechlabs.com/login`
2. Enter your email and password
3. Complete two-factor authentication if enabled
4. Take the guided tour for new users

## AI Analysis Features

### Supported Analysis Types

#### Cavity Detection
- **Purpose**: Identify dental cavities and decay
- **Accuracy**: 95%+ detection rate
- **Input**: High-resolution dental photographs or X-rays
- **Output**: Cavity location, severity, and treatment recommendations

#### Gum Disease Assessment
- **Purpose**: Evaluate gum health and periodontal conditions
- **Features**: Inflammation detection, pocket depth estimation
- **Recommendations**: Treatment plans and preventive measures

#### Tooth Alignment Analysis
- **Purpose**: Assess dental alignment and bite issues
- **Features**: Crowding detection, spacing analysis
- **Output**: Orthodontic recommendations

#### Oral Cancer Screening
- **Purpose**: Early detection of suspicious lesions
- **Features**: Tissue analysis, risk assessment
- **Output**: Risk level and recommended follow-up

### Performing AI Analysis

#### Image Upload and Analysis

1. **Prepare Your Image**
   - Ensure good lighting and clear focus
   - Follow image quality guidelines
   - Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, DICOM

2. **Start Analysis**
   - Click "AI Analysis" in main menu
   - Select "New Analysis"
   - Choose analysis type

3. **Upload Image**
   - Drag and drop image file
   - Or click "Browse" to select file
   - Add patient association
   - Include relevant metadata

4. **Configure Analysis Settings**
   - Select specific tooth regions if applicable
   - Choose analysis sensitivity level
   - Add clinical notes

5. **Run Analysis**
   - Click "Start Analysis"
   - Wait for processing (typically 30-60 seconds)
   - Review results when complete

#### Interpreting Results

##### Result Dashboard
- **Overall Score**: Confidence level of AI analysis
- **Detections**: List of identified conditions
- **Severity Levels**: Color-coded severity indicators
- **Recommendations**: Suggested treatment plans

##### Detailed Analysis View
- **Annotated Images**: Visual markers showing detections
- **Confidence Scores**: Individual confidence for each detection
- **Comparison Tools**: Side-by-side view with previous analyses
- **Export Options**: PDF reports, image downloads

##### Clinical Decision Support
- **Treatment Recommendations**: AI-suggested treatment options
- **Referral Suggestions**: When specialist consultation is recommended
- **Follow-up Timeline**: Suggested monitoring schedule
- **Risk Assessment**: Patient risk factors and considerations

### Quality Assurance

#### Image Quality Requirements

- **Coverage**: Complete view of area of interest

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### Login Problems
**Issue**: Cannot log in to account
**Solutions:**
- Verify email and password
- Check for caps lock
- Try password reset
- Clear browser cache
- Contact support if issues persist

#### Image Upload Issues
**Issue**: Images won't upload or analyze
**Solutions:**
- Check file size (max 50MB)
- Verify file format (JPEG, PNG, DICOM)
- Ensure stable internet connection
- Try different browser
- Check image quality requirements

#### Analysis Errors
**Issue**: AI analysis fails or produces errors
**Solutions:**
- Verify image quality meets requirements
- Check for proper lighting and focus
- Ensure complete coverage of area of interest
- Try re-uploading the image
- Contact support with error details

#### Report Generation Problems
**Issue**: Reports fail to generate or download
**Solutions:**
- Check date range selections
- Verify data availability for selected period
- Try smaller date ranges
- Clear browser cache
- Use different browser or device

## Best Practices

### Image Quality Guidelines

#### Optimal Image Conditions
- **Lighting**: Use consistent, bright lighting
- **Background**: Clean, neutral background
- **Focus**: Ensure sharp focus on area of interest
- **Angle**: Capture from appropriate clinical angle
- **Coverage**: Include complete view of relevant anatomy

#### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Overexposed or underexposed images
- Motion blur or camera shake
- Obstructed views or poor angles
- Inconsistent lighting conditions
- Images with excessive artifacts

### Workflow Optimization

#### Efficient Patient Management
- Complete patient profiles before first analysis
- Use consistent naming conventions
- Regular data backup and archiving
- Maintain up-to-date contact information
- Document all clinical decisions and outcomes

#### AI Analysis Best Practices
- Follow standardized imaging protocols
- Use appropriate analysis types for each case
- Review AI results with clinical expertise
- Document any disagreements with AI findings
- Track accuracy and outcomes over time

#### Data Security
- Regular password updates
- Enable two-factor authentication
- Log out when not in use
- Be cautious with shared devices
- Report any security concerns immediately

### Clinical Integration

- Track outcomes and effectiveness
- Adjust protocols based on results

#### Patient Communication
- Explain AI technology benefits to patients
- Show visual results to enhance understanding
- Discuss AI limitations and clinical judgment
- Maintain patient confidence in technology
- Address any concerns or questions

#### Quality Assurance
- Regular review of AI analysis accuracy

- Continuous improvement processes
